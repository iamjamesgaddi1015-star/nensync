# NenSync

Private daily-work workspace for remote workers. The daily job is to write a standup, choose three priorities, start a focus interval and copy an honest sign-off. English-first; personal accounts, no team sharing. Use only synthetic data in local verification.

Mode: Operate. Approved direction: light rounded dashboard from DESIGN.md and the v2 mockup, with Light/Dark/System, the blue/mint palette, fluid relative units and no sidebar. The timer and numbered three-priority stack carry the identity. No decorative imagery is needed.

The owner explicitly approved PLAN.md v1.2 with “approved. let's proceed.” This authorizes the local implementation/testing stages. Hosting, live Supabase activation/migrations, SMTP and deployment retain separate approval boundaries. No credentials or existing live accounts may be read by agents.
